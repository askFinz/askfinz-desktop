# askFinz Browser: AI desktop app for Windows, macOS, Linux

Its own web browser, driven by the agents, and askFinz Drive keeping your files and your workspace as one. The coder — editor, debugger, terminals — follows on the same subscription.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/desktop](https://askfinz.com/desktop)

## Key points

### The first release, then the coder

The browser and the drive come first, because they are what the desktop is for: a machine of your own that an agent can actually work on, and a folder it can actually read.

### Breakpoints that actually stop

The desktop debugger runs server-side over the Debug Adapter Protocol, with breakpoints we've verified hit live. Set them, step through frames, inspect variables — across more languages than the web IDE.

### What it isn't

The first release is the browser and the drive, with local models and the folder reader. The development environment is built and follows them — we would rather say that than let a download page imply you get all of it on day one.

### Two front doors. Different jobs

Desktop is the surface that runs on hardware you own: a browser an agent can drive without asking anyone's permission, your files on your own disk, and local models for offline, low-latency work.

### Platforms

Code-signing is being finalised. Until then, private-access installers may trigger the operating system's “unverified developer” warning the first time they open.
